## 🎯 **WHAT IT IS**
**Business Rule Extractor Agent AI** - An intelligent system that automatically extracts business requirements from legacy COBOL programs and transforms them into searchable, analyzable knowledge bases with AI-powered modernization recommendations.

## 🚀 **KEY BUSINESS VALUE**

### **For HTC Team & Legacy Modernization:**

### **ROI Impact:**
- **Time Savings**: 80-90% reduction in requirements analysis time
- **Knowledge Preservation**: Comprehensive documentation of legacy business logic
- **Strategic Planning**: Data-driven modernization strategies with risk assessment

## 🔧 **HOW IT WORKS**

### **1. AI-Powered Analysis**
- Upload COBOL files (.cbl, .cob, .cobol)
- Advanced AI agents (OpenAI GPT-4 + Google Gemini) analyze code structure
- Extracts business rules, data definitions, validation logic, and integration points

### **2. Intelligent Knowledge Base**
- Stores extracted requirements in vector database (Qdrant)
- Enables semantic search across all extracted business rules
- Creates searchable repository of legacy system knowledge

### **3. Modernization Intelligence**
- Chain-of-Thought AI prompting for strategic recommendations
- Analyzes business constraints and architecture preferences
- Generates step-by-step modernization roadmaps
- Provides technology mapping and implementation guidance
